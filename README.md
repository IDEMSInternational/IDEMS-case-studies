# STACK personas

Draft personas of the people involved in IDEMS' STACK work: students, teaching staff, technical support, institutional leaders, funders and researchers in African higher education, plus the commercial partner contracts that grew out of that work.

Open `index.html` in a browser to browse the personas.

**Status:** internal draft. Names, details and quotes are fictional composites grounded in the STACK case studies (stack-assessment.org, 2019–2026) and Juma et al. (2022), iJET 17(23).

**Images:** `images/` holds placeholder photos from Wikimedia Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain; credits are listed on the page). They show real, unrelated people and must be replaced with generated or consented images before publication. Each persona includes an image-generation prompt for this.

**Confidentiality:** do not name the US commercial partner in any external version.
