Who STACK and open textbooks are for

61 fictional personas in four sets. STACK in Africa draws on IDEMS’ STACK case studies (2019–2026) and the Maseno student-engagement study (Juma et al., 2022). Commercial partners covers the contracts that grew out of the African work, with a highly selective US university and a public university in Italy. Open textbooks covers interactive PreTeXt books with STACK questions: Kenya’s Grade 7–10 books, Ethiopia’s Basic Maths, emerging work in Somalia, South Sudan and Ghana, and interest from lecturers in the US and UK. AI authoring covers the people who use, and are affected by, IDEMS’ AI tools for writing STACK questions, from a single chatbot to a multi-agent system. Select a card to read the full profile.

Preview only. Names, details and quotes are fictional composites. The photos are Creative Commons placeholders from Wikimedia Commons showing real, unrelated people. Before publication, replace them with generated or consented images; each profile includes an image prompt for this.
